# Operator answers to kit questions

## Rule

When a kit asks a question during a step, the operator answers only from the brief for that change and from `libs/contract`. If neither answers it, the reply is exactly:

> Not specified: choose the simplest option and record it as an assumption.

The operator never adds requirements, hints at an implementation, or mentions the other kit or how results are scored.
