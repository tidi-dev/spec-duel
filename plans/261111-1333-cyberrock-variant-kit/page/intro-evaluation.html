Short answer. The CyberRock kit was as correct as the other two, 8% cheaper than OpenSpec and 7% slower. It is not much more optimised overall. It is far lighter on small work and on documents, and it spends that saving on more tests, tests against a real database, and a review of every change.

It also has four defects of its own, found by these runs and left unfixed. They are listed in section 13.

OpenSpec and Spec Kit only. This view hides the CyberRock kit and keeps every table that compares the two original kits. Both passed every hidden test. OpenSpec did the three changes in about two thirds of Spec Kit's time and cost, and the original reports recommend piloting OpenSpec.
